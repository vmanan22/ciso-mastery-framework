# ADR-006: Evidence-Backed vCISO Engagement Simulator (Cross-Track Capstone)

## Status
**Proposed / Planned (Post-v0.3)**

## Context
In the CLOS capability model, technical controls, infrastructure policies, container attestations, and AI security telemetry provide machine-readable evidence (*Threat ➔ Requirement ➔ Architecture Decision ➔ Control ➔ Implementation ➔ Test ➔ Evidence ➔ Risk Metric ➔ Executive Decision*). 

To bridge the transition from technical engineering to fractional/advisory executive leadership (vCISO), learners need an end-to-end simulated engagement where technical evidence directly drives executive advisory outputs (board presentations, risk registers, budget allocations, and compliance handovers).

## Decision
Design and schedule an **Evidence-Backed vCISO Engagement Simulator** as a post-v0.3 cross-track capstone project.

1. **Cross-Track Positioning**: The simulator acts as a cross-track capstone primarily synthesizing leadership and assurance across 4 canonical tracks:
   - **Track 2 (Security Architecture)**: Architectural threat modeling, trust boundary decomposition, and specification-first controls.
   - **Track 6 (Assurance, Validation, Red Teaming & Pentesting)**: Automated control validation, adversarial test verification, and audit attestation.
   - **Track 7 (Security Operations & Resilience)**: Audit logging, incident telemetry, and operational resilience metrics.
   - **Track 8 (CISO Operations, Enterprise Risk, Budgeting & Board Communication)**: Risk registers, ROSI budget modeling, executive decision memos, and board reporting.

   *Technical Evidence Providers*: Consumes machine-readable evidence produced by **Track 3 (DevSecOps & Secure Software Supply Chain)**, **Track 4 (Cloud Security & Infrastructure as Code)**, and **Track 5 (AI Security & Autonomous Systems)**.

2. **Synthetic Profiles & Data Privacy**:
   - The simulator executes entirely against synthetic fictional organizations (e.g., *Acme FinTech Corp*, *CloudScale Health*).
   - Zero personal, individual-specific, or real-company proprietary data will be stored or referenced.

3. **Artifact Transformation Pipeline**:
   The capstone ingests authentic machine-readable CI/CD scan artifacts (SARIF, Syft SPDX SBOM, OPA Rego evaluation results, Trivy CVE JSON, and ATLAS telemetry JSONL) and synthesizes:
   - Client Engagement Scope & Advisory Charter
   - Evidence-Confidence Assessment Matrix
   - Dynamic Risk Register with quantifiable business impact
   - Prioritized Security Control Remediation Roadmap
   - Budget Allocation & ROSI Trade-Off Scenarios
   - Executive Decision Memo & Board Presentation Deck
   - Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Package
   - Audit-Ready Handover & Assurance Bundle

## Non-Goals
* **No Ninth Track**: CLOS strictly maintains its 8 core capability tracks. The simulator is a cross-track synthesis capstone, not an independent track.
* **No Premature Implementation**: No placeholder code or runtime components will be scaffolded before Milestone v0.3 is complete.
* **No Mocking of Technical Evidence**: The simulator must consume authentic, schema-valid evidence generated by v0.1, v0.2, and v0.3 pipelines.

## Prerequisites
1. **v0.1**: Flagship Secure Software Factory providing fail-closed CI/CD evidence (SARIF, SBOM, OPA evaluation).
2. **v0.2**: Zero-Trust AI Security Gateway providing runtime MITRE ATLAS telemetry and policy enforcement logs.
3. **v0.3**: Technical CISO Control Plane providing automated DAST results and machine-readable OSCAL records.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] End-to-end simulated workflow executable locally using standard scripts.
- [ ] Synthetic organization dataset covers realistic regulatory and technical constraints (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA).
- [ ] Direct automated mapping from technical scanner JSON/SARIF outputs to board memo risk figures.
- [ ] Zero unverified claims or hardcoded assumptions in generated advisory deliverables.
