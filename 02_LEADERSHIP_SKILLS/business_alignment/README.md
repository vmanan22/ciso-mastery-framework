# Business Alignment

> Align security initiatives with business goals and speak the language of the business.

---

## Learning Objectives

- [ ] Translate security investments into business value (ROI, risk reduction)
- [ ] Map security controls to business processes and revenue streams
- [ ] Build business cases for security initiatives
- [ ] Understand financial concepts relevant to security (CAPEX vs OPEX, TCO, cost of breach)
- [ ] Align security roadmap with company strategy
- [ ] Measure and report security metrics that matter to the business
- [ ] Navigate organizational politics to advance security initiatives

---

## Frameworks

### Security as a Business Enabler
| Security Capability | Business Enablement |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| SOC 2 compliance | Accelerate enterprise sales |
| Data encryption | Enter regulated markets |
| Secure SDLC | Reduce production incidents → faster delivery |
| Incident response | Protect brand reputation |
| Privacy controls | Build customer trust |

### Business Case Template
1. **Problem Statement** — What business problem does this solve?
2. **Current State** — What's the cost of the current situation?
3. **Proposed Solution** — What are we proposing?
4. **Investment Required** — How much does it cost?
5. **Expected Return** — What's the ROI / risk reduction?
6. **Timeline** — When will we see results?
7. **Risks** — What could go wrong?
