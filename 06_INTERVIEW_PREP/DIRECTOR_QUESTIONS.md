# Director of Security Engineering Interview Questions

> 20 real-world questions for Senior Director / VP of Security Engineering roles.
> These are more hands-on and technical leadership focused than CISO questions.

---

## Technical Leadership

### Q1: How do you set technical direction for a security engineering team?
**What they're testing:** Technical vision, team alignment
**Framework hint:** Tech radar, ADRs, architecture reviews, innovation time

### Q2: Describe how you've scaled a security engineering team from 5 to 20+ people.
**What they're testing:** Organizational design, hiring, team structure
**Framework hint:** Specialization, on-call, career ladders, hiring bar

### Q3: How do you balance building custom security tools vs. buying commercial solutions?
**What they're testing:** Build vs. buy thinking, resource allocation
**Framework hint:** TCO, maintenance burden, competitive advantage, time-to-value

### Q4: Walk me through your approach to technical debt in security systems.
**What they're testing:** Pragmatism, prioritization
**Framework hint:** Risk-ranked backlog, allocated sprint capacity, metrics

---

## Application Security

### Q5: How would you build an AppSec program from scratch for a fast-moving startup?
**What they're testing:** Program design, pragmatism, prioritization
**Framework hint:** OWASP SAMM, quick wins first, developer experience focus

### Q6: How do you embed security into a development team's workflow without slowing them down?
**What they're testing:** DevSecOps philosophy, developer empathy
**Framework hint:** Shift left, guardrails not gates, fast feedback, self-service

### Q7: What's your approach to managing vulnerabilities at scale (1000+ findings)?
**What they're testing:** Triage, prioritization, operational efficiency
**Framework hint:** CVSS + context, SLA-based remediation, automation, exceptions

### Q8: How do you evaluate the security of a codebase you've never seen before?
**What they're testing:** Code review skills, systematic approach
**Framework hint:** Architecture → AuthN/AuthZ → data flows → dependencies → hotspots

---

## Infrastructure & Cloud Security

### Q9: Design a secure container platform for 500 microservices.
**What they're testing:** K8s security, architecture at scale
**Framework hint:** Multi-tenant, RBAC, network policies, image scanning, runtime protection

### Q10: How do you implement infrastructure-as-code with security guardrails?
**What they're testing:** IaC security, policy-as-code
**Framework hint:** OPA/Rego, Terraform scanning, CI gates, drift detection

### Q11: What's your strategy for secrets management across multiple environments?
**What they're testing:** Practical security engineering
**Framework hint:** Vault/KMS, rotation, injection patterns, audit, zero-trust secrets

### Q12: How would you detect and respond to a compromised CI/CD pipeline?
**What they're testing:** Supply chain security, detection engineering
**Framework hint:** SLSA, provenance, anomaly detection, immutable builds, audit logs

---

## Security Operations & Detection

### Q13: How do you build a detection engineering program?
**What they're testing:** SOC maturity, detection-as-code
**Framework hint:** MITRE ATT&CK coverage, detection-as-code, purple teaming, metrics

### Q14: Describe your approach to logging and monitoring at scale.
**What they're testing:** Observability, cost management, signal vs. noise
**Framework hint:** Centralized logging, alerting tiers, SLOs, cost optimization

### Q15: How do you measure the effectiveness of your security engineering efforts?
**What they're testing:** Metrics-driven leadership
**Framework hint:** MTTD, MTTR, vulnerability SLA compliance, coverage metrics

---

## Cross-Functional & Leadership

### Q16: How do you handle a situation where a product team ships a feature with known security issues?
**What they're testing:** Conflict resolution, risk acceptance process
**Framework hint:** Risk register, compensating controls, documented acceptance, SLA

### Q17: Describe a time you had to make a difficult trade-off between security and business velocity.
**What they're testing:** Pragmatism, business awareness
**Framework hint:** STAR, risk quantification, stakeholder alignment

### Q18: How do you ensure your team stays current with emerging threats and technologies?
**What they're testing:** Continuous learning, team development
**Framework hint:** Learning budget, conferences, internal tech talks, threat intel, CTFs

### Q19: How do you work with your CISO to translate strategy into execution?
**What they're testing:** Upward management, strategic execution
**Framework hint:** OKRs, roadmap alignment, regular syncs, metrics reporting

### Q20: Where do you see security engineering in 5 years, and how are you preparing?
**What they're testing:** Vision, forward thinking
**Framework hint:** AI/ML security, platform engineering, developer-first security, automation
